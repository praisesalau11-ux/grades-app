🚀 Getting Started

GradeFlow is an academic management tool designed to help students calculate GPA, organise academic records, monitor performance and manage their study activities.

Sign in or create an account, complete your profile if requested, and use the dashboard to access the available academic tools.

The dashboard provides access to GradeFlow features such as the GPA calculator, subjects, history, analytics, goals, reports, study planning and account settings.

🧮 GPA Calculator

Open the GPA Calculator, enter your subjects, grades and credit units, then use the calculate option to see your result. Make sure your grading scale matches your school.

A credit unit represents the academic weight assigned to a course. GPA calculations use credit units to determine how much each course contributes to the result.

GradeFlow provides grading-scale settings. Select the scale that matches your institution before calculating your GPA. Using the wrong scale can produce an inaccurate result.

Check your grades, credit units, grading scale and rounding method. Your institution may also apply rules that are not included in your calculator settings.

📚 Academic Tools

Open Subjects & Semesters from the dashboard. Use the available controls to organise your academic subjects and semester information.

Study goals help you define academic targets and monitor your progress towards them.

The Study Planner is designed to help you organise your study activities and create a more structured study routine.

These tools are intended to help you identify areas to work on and organise actions that can improve your academic performance.

📊 Records & Analytics

Open History from the dashboard to view saved calculation records. You can use the available search and date filters to find records.

The Performance page presents academic information such as GPA trends, latest results, subject performance and saved records, depending on the data available in your account.

Analytics helps you understand your academic information. Reports are intended to present your academic records in a more organised format.

👤 Account & Settings

Open My Profile from the dashboard and use the available profile controls to update your personal or academic details.

Open Settings and use the theme preference if it is available in your version of GradeFlow.

Use the password reset option on the login page or in Settings if available. Follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

If the export option is available in your Settings page, use it to export your saved academic history.

💬 Community & AI

The AI Academic Assistant is designed to help with academic questions and learning-related tasks. Always verify important academic information.

Study Groups are intended to help students connect and collaborate around their academic activities.

Use the Messages and Notifications sections from the dashboard to access those features.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Check your internet connection, refresh the page and try again. If the problem continues, try another browser or contact support with details about the problem.

Confirm that you are signed in to the correct account. Check your internet connection and refresh the page. If records remain missing, contact support.

Contact GradeFlow support through WhatsApp or email. Describe what you were doing, what happened, and what you expected to happen. Include a screenshot if useful.
